---

## How to read this list

- **Tier 1 = Jeddah / Western Region HQ** — your priority. Decision-makers are in your city; you can visit the head office or flagship store in person.
- **Tier 2 = Saudi & regional chains** with strong Jeddah branches — HQ elsewhere, but Jeddah area managers can buy.
- **Tier 3 = Brand leads under the big retail groups** — each brand has its own visual-merchandising (VM) / procurement contact even when the parent is shared. One good relationship with a group VM department can supply 10+ brands.

**Who to contact:** For Tiers 1–2: the owner/GM (small chains) or Visual Merchandising / Procurement Manager (larger ones) — find them via LinkedIn, or simply walk into the Jeddah flagship and ask the store manager who buys their display equipment. For Tier 3: search LinkedIn for "*Visual Merchandising* + [group name] + Saudi".

**The pitch:** replacement + expansion. Chains constantly open branches and replace damaged mannequins. Offer: bulk pricing (5+), delivery to any branch in KSA, and consistent restock of the same model — that consistency is exactly what a chain wants and what cheap importers can't give them.

## Working the List (suggested order)

1. **Week 1–2: #1–26 (Jeddah tier).** Start with #16 Landmark Arabia and #4–5 KOJ Group — both headquartered in Jeddah, both massive. For the rest, visit flagships in Red Sea Mall / Mall of Arabia and ask for the person who buys display equipment; leave a printed one-page catalog with wholesale pricing.
2. **Week 3–4: abaya & thobe chains (#6–24).** These are your natural fit: Saudi-owned, Western-region, mid-size — decisions are made by one owner, fast.
3. **Month 2: Tier 2, then Tier 3 group VM departments** (#48, 55, 66, 74, 88, 92) via LinkedIn. One approved-vendor registration with a group can be worth 50 stores.

**Qualifying question for every lead (your own rule):** "How many branches do you currently operate?" — if the answer is 1, deprioritize; 2+ proceed.

**Track it simply:** add three columns when you copy this into Google Sheets — *Contacted (date) / Response / Next step*. Five contacts per week from this list = the full Tier 1 covered in a month.

### Honesty notes
- Branch counts are from public sources as of July 2026 and shift constantly — treat them as scale indicators, not exact figures.
- Franchise operator mappings (which group runs which brand in KSA) change hands; rows marked *(verify operator)* should be confirmed on LinkedIn before you address the pitch.
- No phone numbers/emails are listed on purpose — scraped contacts go stale and hurt deliverability. The reliable path: brand website → "Contact/Wholesale", LinkedIn (VM/procurement roles), or the flagship store manager in person.
