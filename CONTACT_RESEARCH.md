## ⚠️ Read this first: about personal mobile numbers

Personal mobile numbers of VM/procurement managers are **not published anywhere legitimate**. Sites like Lusha, ZoomInfo and RocketReach hold them behind paywalls, and their Saudi data is frequently stale or wrong — cold-calling a wrong or scraped personal number burns the lead and your reputation. Also, several of these brands' websites actively block automated data collection (I hit bot-walls on lomar.sa, rubaiyat.com, alhijab.sa).

**What actually works in Saudi B2B — and what this document gives you:**
1. **Verified company numbers** (unified 9200-numbers, WhatsApp lines, head-office addresses) — these are answered by humans who will transfer you.
2. **The exact job title that buys mannequins** at each company size — so you ask for the right person by title.
3. **Ready-made LinkedIn searches + scripts** — in Saudi retail, the mobile number comes from the first conversation, not before it. Two polite steps and you'll have the decision-maker's WhatsApp.

**Who buys mannequins (this matters):**
- **Small chains (2–15 branches):** the **owner/founder or General Manager** decides personally. Titles to ask for: صاحب المؤسسة / المدير العام.
- **Mid-size chains (15–60 branches):** **Visual Merchandising Manager** or **Retail Operations Manager**.
- **Big groups (Landmark, Cenomi, Alshaya, Apparel, KOJ):** mannequins sit under **"non-trade / indirect procurement"** or **Store Development / Fit-out** — NOT the merchandise buying team. Ask for: *Non-Trade Procurement* / *Store Development* / *Head of Visual Merchandising*. (Note: Inditex brands like Zara receive display specs from Spain — pitch the local team for **replacement stock**, not new-store fit-outs.)

## The 2-Step Method to Get Any Decision-Maker's Number

**Step 1 — the switchboard script (works on every unified 9200 number above):**
> «السلام عليكم، معك [اسمك] من شركة أموي للمانيكانات في جدة — نحن مصنّع/مورد محلي لمانيكانات العرض. أبغى أوصل لقسم المشتريات أو قسم العرض المرئي (الفيجوال ميرتشندايزنج) بخصوص توريد للفروع. ممكن تحويلي أو تعطيني إيميل/واتساب المسؤول؟»

Receptionists at Saudi retailers transfer supplier calls routinely — this is a normal request. If they offer only an email, send the catalog PDF and follow up by phone in 3 days ("أرسلت الكتالوج، حاب أتأكد وصل للشخص المناسب").

**Step 2 — the store-manager route (for anything with a Jeddah branch):**
Walk into the flagship on a weekday morning (Sun–Thu, 10:00–12:00 — before the manager gets busy), buy nothing, ask:
> «من المسؤول عندكم عن شراء المانيكانات وتجهيز الفروع؟ عندي عرض توريد محلي وأبغى أوصله له.»

Store managers know exactly who reorders display equipment and will usually share the area manager's or fit-out coordinator's WhatsApp on the spot — mannequins breaking is *their* daily problem and a local supplier helps *them*.

**LinkedIn DM template (for the VM/procurement people you find via the search links):**
> Hi [name], I run AMOY Mannequins, a Jeddah-based supplier of retail mannequins & display forms (full-body, torso, kids — 90–1,450 SAR). We hold local stock and deliver kingdom-wide in 48h, which means no 3-month import lead times when branches need replacements. Could I send you our trade catalog and bulk pricing? Happy to drop samples at your office.

**Timing:** Saudi B2B answers Sun–Thu; best windows 10:00–13:00 and 19:30–22:00 (retail people). Avoid Friday entirely and Ramadan daytime.
