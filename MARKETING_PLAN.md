## 2. Who Actually Buys Mannequins (Your Real Customer)

Nobody impulse-buys a mannequin. Your buyers are:

1. **New clothing/abaya/boutique store owners** setting up a shop (buy 3–10 at once)
2. **Existing stores** replacing broken or refreshing displays (buy 1–3)
3. **Tailors & abaya designers** (torso/bust forms, 390–590 SAR)
4. **Online sellers / Instagram boutiques** who photograph clothes on forms (bust forms, 190–390 SAR)
5. **Shopfitting & interior design companies** furnishing retail projects (bulk, repeat)
6. **Event/exhibition organizers** (rent or buy short-term)

They search in **Arabic**, mostly on **Google, Haraj, and Instagram**, and they want to **WhatsApp you before buying**. The whole plan is built around meeting them there.

## 4. One-Time Setup Week (do once, ~6–8 hours total, spread over days)

### Day 1 — Google Business Profile (biggest single win)
- Create a profile at business.google.com for **"AMOY Mannequins | أموي للمانيكانات"**.
- Category: *Mannequin supplier / Store fixture supplier*. Add your city, phone, WhatsApp, website, hours.
- Upload 10–15 real photos of mannequins (in your warehouse/showroom if possible — real photos rank better than renders).
- Write the description in Arabic first, English second. Include: مانيكان، مانيكانات، ستاند عرض ملابس، مانيكان نسائي، مانيكان رجالي، مانيكان أطفال، توصيل لجميع مدن المملكة.
- After every future sale, send the customer your review link — 5 reviews puts you above most competitors.

### Day 2 — Haraj + OpenSooq (free classifieds = buyer intent)
- Post 5–8 listings on **haraj.com.sa** (category: أثاث تجاري / معدات محلات). One listing per product family, not per SKU:
  - مانيكان نسائي كامل (from 890 SAR) · مانيكان رجالي (from 890) · مانيكان أطفال (from 650) · ستاند تورسو للخياطين والمصممين (from 390) · أطقم كاملة لتجهيز المحلات
- Each listing: 3–4 photos, price, "توصيل لجميع المدن", your WhatsApp, and the store link.
- Repeat the same on **opensooq.com**. Refresh/re-up listings every 2 weeks (5 minutes).

### Day 3 — WhatsApp Business + store fixes
- Set up **WhatsApp Business** with a catalog (add your top 10 products with SAR prices).
- Add a **floating WhatsApp chat button** to the Shopify store (free apps: "WhatsApp Chat" — pick any with 4.5★+). Saudi B2B buyers want to negotiate quantity and confirm delivery before paying; without this button they just leave. This alone likely explains 6 carts → 0 orders.
- Set a WhatsApp greeting + away message in Arabic (template in §8).
- On the store: confirm shipping costs/times to Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam are stated clearly on every product page, and add a "طلبات الجملة — Bulk Orders" line: *"5+ pieces? WhatsApp us for a wholesale price."*

### Day 4 — Arabic SEO (compounds forever)
- Your product titles are English-only. Add Arabic to each **product page description** (first paragraph) and to **SEO title/meta**: e.g. *"مانيكان نسائي كامل – وقفة ثابتة | Female Full-Body Mannequin (FA-02) | توصيل لجميع مدن السعودية"*.
- Create/verify these **collections** with Arabic-titled landing copy: مانيكان نسائي · مانيكان رجالي · مانيكان أطفال · ستاندات تورسو وأجزاء · تجهيز محلات.
- Target keywords (put them naturally in titles, descriptions, alt text):
  - مانيكان للبيع · مانيكانات الرياض / جدة / الدمام · مانيكان نسائي · مانيكان رجالي · مانيكان اطفال · ستاند عرض ملابس · تجهيز محلات ملابس · مانيكان خياطة · اجسام عرض
- Add one evergreen **blog/page**: "دليل اختيار المانيكان المناسب لمحلك" (How to choose the right mannequin for your store) — 600–800 words, link to collections. This is your long-term Google magnet.
- Submit the sitemap in **Google Search Console** (amoylimited.com/sitemap.xml).

### Day 5 — Google Merchant Center free listings
- Connect Shopify → **Google & YouTube channel** app → Merchant Center → enable **free listings**. Your 42 products then appear in the Google Shopping tab for free. Zero maintenance after setup.

### Day 6 — Social accounts (setup only, content comes later)
- Instagram + TikTok + Snapchat as **@amoymannequins** (Snapchat matters in KSA). Bio in Arabic: what you sell, cities you deliver to, WhatsApp link, store link.
- Post an initial grid of 6–9 product photos so the account doesn't look empty.

## 5. The Weekly Routine (~30 minutes, that's it)

**Every week, same 30 minutes:**

1. **(10 min) Outreach ×5:** Find 5 new/renovating clothing stores — Instagram hashtags like #افتتاح_محل, #بوتيك_جديد, Google Maps "recently opened," or just boutiques with weak displays — and send the DM template in §8. Five DMs a week = ~20/month; at even a 5% close rate on a product every store needs, that's 1 order/month from outreach alone.
2. **(10 min) Post 1 piece of content** (from the monthly batch, §6) to Instagram + TikTok + Snapchat simultaneously.
3. **(5 min) Answer WhatsApp/DM inquiries** you haven't gotten to.
4. **(5 min) Every other week:** refresh Haraj/OpenSooq listings so they float back to the top.

**Once a month (1 hour):** batch-shoot content (§6) and glance at the numbers (§7).

---

## 6. Content: Batch 1 Hour/Month, Post Weekly

Shoot 4–5 clips/photo sets in one session at your warehouse. Phone camera is fine — B2B buyers care about the product, not production value. Rotate these formats:

1. **"Unboxing/assembly in 30 seconds"** — timelapse of a mannequin being assembled. (These perform absurdly well; oddly satisfying content.)
2. **Before/after** — empty rail or floor pile vs. clothes displayed on your mannequin. Caption: "الفرق واضح 👀"
3. **Lineup/range shot** — female, male, kids side by side with SAR prices on screen. Directly shoppable.
4. **Customer install** — whenever you deliver, ask to film 15 seconds in the store (offer a small discount for permission). This is social proof + content in one.
5. **FAQ clip** — "كم سعر المانيكان؟" / "هل يوجد توصيل للرياض؟" — answer on camera in 20 seconds.

Every post: Arabic caption, price or "الأسعار تبدأ من...", and "للطلب واتساب — الرابط في البايو". Hashtags: #مانيكان #تجهيز_محلات #محلات_ملابس #بوتيك #الرياض #جدة #مشروع_ملابس

## 7. How You'll Know It's Working (5 minutes/month)

Check in Shopify Analytics (or ask Claude to pull these numbers):

| Metric | Now | Month 1 target | Month 3 target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Saudi sessions / month | 42 | 150 | 300+ |
| WhatsApp/DM inquiries / month | ~0 | 8–10 | 20+ |
| Orders / month (store + WhatsApp) | 0 | 1–2 | **3–5** |
| Google Business Profile reviews | 0 | 2 | 6+ |

**Count WhatsApp orders as orders.** In Saudi B2B, many buyers will pay by transfer after a WhatsApp chat instead of checking out online — create these as **Draft Orders** in Shopify so your numbers stay accurate.

If after 6 weeks inquiries are coming but orders aren't → it's a pricing/shipping-cost objection; fix that (e.g., free delivery over 2,000 SAR). If inquiries aren't coming → double down on Haraj + outreach, they're the shortest path.

## 9. What NOT to Spend Time On (for now)

- **Paid ads** — you asked for organic; revisit only after organic proves 3 orders/month.
- **Daily posting** — weekly is enough for B2B; consistency beats volume.
- **English-first content** — your buyer searches in Arabic.
- **Chasing the US "traffic"** — it's bots. Ignore that number; track **Saudi sessions** only.
- **More SKUs** — 42 products is plenty; distribution is the bottleneck, not catalog.

1. **One setup week** (Google Business Profile, Haraj/OpenSooq, WhatsApp button, Arabic SEO, Merchant Center, socials).
2. **30 minutes weekly** (5 outreach DMs, 1 post, answer WhatsApp, refresh listings biweekly).
3. **1 hour monthly** (batch content + check the 4 numbers in §7).

The three highest-probability sources of your first 3 monthly orders: **Haraj listings**, **Google Maps**, and **direct DMs to new stores** — all free, all set up in week one.
